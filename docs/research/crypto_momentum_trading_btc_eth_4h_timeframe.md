### Key Insight
Momentum trading in volatile crypto markets like BTC and ETH on a 4-hour timeframe can capture significant trends if managed with strict risk controls. The 4h timeframe balances responsiveness to trends with filtering out the noise of lower timeframes, making it a popular choice for systematic crypto traders.

### The Math
This strategy relies on the relationship between a faster-moving and slower-moving price average. The core signal is generated by a **Dual Moving Average Crossover**.
- `Signal = SMA(short_period) > SMA(long_period)` -> Long Entry / Bullish Trend
- `Signal = SMA(short_period) < SMA(long_period)` -> Exit Long / Bearish Trend

The performance of such a strategy is deeply tied to the principles of [[compounding_daily_returns_math_behind_doubling_a_small_accou|compounding returns]].

### Strategy Logic
1.  **Asset & Timeframe**: Select BTC/USD or ETH/USD on the 4-hour chart.
2.  **Indicators**: Use a short-term Exponential Moving Average (e.g., 12-period EMA) and a long-term EMA (e.g., 26-period EMA). EMA is often preferred over SMA for its faster reaction to recent price changes.
3.  **Entry Signal**: Initiate a long position when the short-term EMA crosses decisively above the long-term EMA.
4.  **Exit Signal**: Close the long position when the short-term EMA crosses back below the long-term EMA.
5.  **Filter**: A critical component is filtering for market regime. This strategy performs best in clear uptrends. One can apply a filter from [[combining_trend_following_with_volatility_filters_for_max_re]] to avoid taking signals during low-volatility, sideways markets where this strategy is prone to "whipsaw" losses.
6.  **Position Sizing**: Determine position size using a fixed fractional model or a more advanced method from [[quantitative_risk_management_position_sizing]].

### Parameters
- **Assets**: BTC/USD, ETH/USD
- **Timeframe**: 4-hour (H4)
- **Short MA**: 12-period EMA
- **Long MA**: 26-period EMA
- **Stop Loss**: A multiple of Average True Range (ATR), such as 2x ATR below the entry price, is a common technique. The ideas in [[scalping_high_volatility_stocks_with_tight_stop_losses]] can be adapted for managing risk in volatile assets.

### Risks
- **Whipsaw**: In ranging or consolidating markets, frequent crossovers can lead to numerous small, compounding losses. This is the primary weakness of this strategy.
- **Flash Crashes**: The crypto market is susceptible to sudden, deep, and rapid price drops that can trigger stops at unfavorable prices before the EMA crossover exit signal occurs.
- **Lack of Edge in Choppy Markets**: This is a trend-following strategy. By definition, it has no edge and will lose money in non-trending, sideways, or "choppy" market conditions.
