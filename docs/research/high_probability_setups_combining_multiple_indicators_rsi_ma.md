# High-Probability Mean Reversion Setup

## Key Insight
This strategy combines [[Bollinger Bands]], [[RSI]], and [[MACD]] to identify high-probability [[mean reversion]] opportunities. The core idea is to find points where an asset is statistically oversold and showing signs of a momentum shift, increasing the probability of a successful reversal trade. It uses [[Bollinger Bands]] to define the probable range of prices, [[RSI]] to confirm oversold/overbought conditions, and [[MACD]] to signal a shift in [[momentum]].

## The Math
- **[[Bollinger Bands]]**:
  - Middle Band: $MB = \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=1}^{N} P_i$ (A simple [[moving average]])
  - Standard Deviation: $\sigma = \sqrt{\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N}(P_i - MB)^2}{N}}$
  - Upper Band: $UB = MB + k \cdot \sigma$
  - Lower Band: $LB = MB - k \cdot \sigma$

- **[[Relative Strength Index (RSI)]]**:
  - Relative Strength: $RS = \frac{\text{Exponentially Weighted MA of Gains}}{\text{Exponentially Weighted MA of Losses}}$
  - Index: $RSI = 100 - \frac{100}{1 + RS}$

- **[[Moving Average Convergence Divergence (MACD)]]**:
  - MACD Line: $EMA_{fast}(Close) - EMA_{slow}(Close)$
  - Signal Line: $EMA_{signal}(MACD Line)$
  - Histogram: $MACD Line - Signal Line$

## Strategy Logic
A potential long entry signal is generated when the following conditions are met in sequence:
1. The price closes below the lower [[Bollinger Bands|Bollinger Band]], indicating a statistically oversold state and high [[volatility]].
2. The [[RSI]] is below the oversold threshold (e.g., 30), confirming the asset has weak [[momentum]].
3. Wait for the [[MACD]] line to cross above the signal line, indicating a potential reversal and shift in [[momentum]]. This is the trigger.
4. Enter the trade. Stop-loss could be placed below the recent low. Profit target could be the middle [[Bollinger Bands|Bollinger Band]] (the [[moving average]]).

## Parameters
- **[[Bollinger Bands]]**:
  - Period (N): 20
  - Standard Deviations (k): 2
- **[[RSI]]**:
  - Period: 14
  - Oversold Threshold: 30
  - Overbought Threshold: 70
- **[[MACD]]**:
  - Fast EMA: 12
  - Slow EMA: 26
  - Signal EMA: 9

## Risks
- **Strong Trends**: In a strong downtrend, a security can trade below the lower [[Bollinger Bands|Bollinger Band]] for an extended period, leading to multiple false signals.
- **[[Volatility]] Expansion**: The bands widen during high [[volatility]], what seems oversold can become more oversold.
- **Parameter Sensitivity**: The strategy's performance is sensitive to the chosen parameters. What works for one asset may not work for another.
- **[[Whipsaw]]**: The [[MACD]] crossover can provide a late signal or be part of a [[whipsaw]] in a ranging market.
